Brand Mark · v1

Agentorist — The Compass.

A bold capital "A" bisecting a four-point compass rose. The apex points North, the crossbar extends out as East and West needles, and a small needle hangs from the inner V notch as South. Letterform and wayfinder fused into one mark. An Agentorist is one whose vocation is finding things on behalf of agents — a compass is the right metaphor.

The mark

Master · monochrome

Construction

100 × 100 grid

How it's built

  • N — Apex: single point at (50, 10). The chevron's apex doubles as the North needle.
  • E / W — Crossbar wings: the bar of the A breaks out of the legs at y = 42–58 and tapers to points at (96, 50) and (4, 50).
  • S — Inner needle: a small triangle anchored inside the V notch at (47, 68)–(53, 68), descending to a point at (50, 84) — visible through the open base of the A.
  • Crossbar: 12-unit-thick bar at y = 44–56, sitting above the V valley so the inner negative space stays clean.
  • Cardinal balance: the four needle tips sit on a perfect cross — N at y = 10, S tip at y = 84 (visually centred on the body), E and W at the y = 50 horizon line.

Three filled SVG paths, plain nonzero winding, no masking or clipping. Renders identically everywhere from cairo to a 1996 browser.
