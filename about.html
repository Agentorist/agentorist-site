About Agentorist

The booking layer
for AI agents.

Every major AI assistant is becoming agentic. Users are starting to say "book me two tickets for tonight" — and the agent has nowhere to go. Agentorist is the layer that takes them there.

The mission

Make the entire world of bookable inventory accessible to AI agents through a single, open protocol. Experiences, events, hotels, restaurants, appointments — anything that can be booked, through one connection.

Built on the Model Context Protocol so any compatible agent (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor, Cline, and the rest) can plug in and immediately give its users the ability to discover, route, and book — without a browser, without a checkout, without friction.

What we believe

Booking is plumbing. Every consumer-facing AI product will need it. Almost none will want to build it.

Honesty over hallucination. When we can't book something, we say so — and we give the user the fastest path to do it themselves. We log every gap so that we can work to close it.

The two-sided product

For users: a booking experience that lives inside the conversation they're already having with their AI assistant. No app to install, no account to create.

For operators: quantified missed-demand data — the questions AI agents are asking about your venue that you didn't know existed — plus a direct integration path when you're ready to capture that demand.

Where we are

Agentorist is an early-stage tech start-up. Experiences are live now through Viator's global tour catalogue, and events through Ticketmaster. Hotels, appointments, and restaurants are landing through 2026. The discovery, demand-intelligence, and routing layers are already running.

If you're a venue, operator, or developer who wants to be part of the first booking layer purpose-built for the agentic era — get in touch.
