Trust & Safety

A trusted intermediary, not an open door.

When AI agents start booking on behalf of millions of people, new risks appear — poisoned data hijacking the agent, scalper bots wearing AI masks, scraping at machine speed, personal data leaking to places it shouldn't. Agentorist runs a 13-control trust stack across three layers that protects our booking partners, the AI agents that connect to us, and the people those agents act for.

Layer 1 — Inbound

We know who's calling.

Every AI client is identified and rate-limited if necessary to prevent excessive API calling. Inbound queries are sanitised against potential prompt-injection before anything else happens.

Layer 2 — Processing

Nothing dirty gets through.

Responses are sanitised, booking links whitelisted to verified partner domains, personal data filtered, scraping and low-intent bookings detected — every event audited.

Layer 3 — Outbound

A good citizen to partners.

We self-throttle, never leak internal errors, and preserve every partner's tracking intact. Partners get clean, attributed, intent-verified traffic.

Why

Booking platforms are right to be wary of anonymous AI traffic. We're the buffer that makes it safe to say yes — and a clear reason for partners to work with us rather than around us.
