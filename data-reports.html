Every time someone asks an AI agent to book your venue and it can't, we log it. Request a report and see how many users are quietly walking away.

What a report includes

A demand report shows, for a given venue or venue chain in a chosen region, over a chosen time window:

· Total number of times users asked an AI agent to book your venue
· Distinct user count (so you can tell repeated demand from breadth)
· The agent client behind each request (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, others)
· Specific items / showtimes / dates users tried to book
· Top locations and time-of-day patterns
· Last-seen timestamp per venue

Reports are delivered as a structured PDF plus a CSV of the raw rows. No personally identifiable information is included unless a user has explicitly opted in.

Most operators move from a one-off report to a monthly subscription once they see the data. Subscription reports include trend analysis, week-over-week deltas, and venue-specific recommendations. Mention "monthly" in the notes field above and we'll include pricing in our reply.
